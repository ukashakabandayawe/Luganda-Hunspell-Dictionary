This document contains the flags used with transitive verbs only.
After analyzing the type-I and type-II stems, the difference in the flags only happens in origial stems, modified stems, and past tense stems. The rest are the same. 
Therefore, this document overrides both the type-I stems common flags (resources\Type-I stems common flags.txt) and type-II stems common flags documents (resources\Type-II stems common flags.txt). 

Any verb that is compatible with flag NN and flag UU without changing the meaning is a Type-II.
For example, the verb "okusiga"(to sow/plant) takes the flags NN and UU, but the meaning changes which makes it a Type-I stem. 
i.e Tuzisiga(we plant them) vs. Tuzeesiga(we trust them).
There are some verbs that are easy to identify as Type-I because they are compatible with flag NN and not compatible with flag UU. 
e.g "okufumba"(to cook) it makes sense to say "tubifumba" (after adding flag NN) but it does not make sense to say "tubyefumba"(after adding flag UU). 
An example of a Type-II verb is "okusiiga"(to smear/paint) it makes sense to say "Tubisiiga"(we paint them) and "Tubyesiiga"(we paint them to ourselves).
